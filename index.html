Set up your 5 players and their champion pools. Drag a champion from a pool into the tier list to rank it for that player — it gets a role tag (TOP, JGL, MID, BOT, SUP), so the same champion can be ranked once per player. The badge on a pool champion shows its tier; drag a tagged champion back here to unrank it. Tip: double-click a champion or pair to send it straight to a tier. Use the eye button on a pool to hide its entries on the tier list without losing them.
